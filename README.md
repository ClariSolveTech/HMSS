# Aura Health OS

You are a Senior UI/UX Designer, Frontend Architect, and Product Designer with experience designing products for Google, Apple, Stripe, Linear, Notion, and Vercel.

Design and build a premium, modern, enterprise-grade landing website for an AI-powered Healthcare Operating System.

The product is NOT a traditional Hospital Management System.

It is an AI-powered Healthcare Operating System that helps hospitals manage operations while continuously caring for patients even after they leave the hospital through AI reminders, voice calls, smart follow-ups, and intelligent healthcare automation.

The design should immediately communicate trust, innovation, healthcare, and simplicity.

The website should look like a billion-dollar healthcare startup.
